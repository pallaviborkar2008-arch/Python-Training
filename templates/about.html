💡 About Student Quiz Hub

Everything students need for effective quiz practice.

Student Quiz Hub provides a simple, attractive and interactive environment where students can practice programming concepts through MCQ-based quizzes. Students can select subjects, attempt quizzes, view detailed results, check their performance on the leaderboard and use the AI Study Assistant to clear their doubts.

🎯 Why Student Quiz Hub?

Designed to make learning easier and more engaging.

  • ✅ Easy and student-friendly interface
  • 📝 Practice with subject-wise quizzes
  • 📊 Get instant score and percentage
  • 🏆 Compare performance through leaderboard
  • 🤖 Clear doubts using AI Study Assistant
  • 📚 Get study tips after completing quizzes
  • 🚀 Improve knowledge through regular practice
